Put the team app on Railway

One app for everyone. You get an owner dashboard; each agent gets a card + their own dashboard. ~15 minutes, no coding.

💡 You do not need a database. This app stores everything in a file on a Railway Volume (one click, below). Keep the Volume and your data is safe across updates.

Part 1 — Get the code onto GitHub

1Go to github.com/new and create a repository (name it meridian-team). Keep it Private. Click Create.
2On the new repo page, click uploading an existing file. Drag in everything inside the meridian-team folder (server.js, package.json, the lib, public folders, etc.). Click Commit changes.
On a phone? Easiest is to do this part on a computer. If you can't, send me the folder and I can help you get it into GitHub, or we use Railway's CLI instead — just ask.

Part 2 — Deploy on Railway

3Go to railway.app and sign in with the account your boss gave you.
4Click New Project → Deploy from GitHub repo → choose meridian-team. Railway builds it automatically (it reads package.json and runs it). Wait for the green Deployed.
5Add storage so nothing is lost. In the service, click Variables tab is next; first open the Settings → Volumes (or right-click the service → Attach Volume). Create a volume and set the Mount path to: /data
6Open the Variables tab and add these (click New Variable for each): DATA_DIR = /data OWNER_EMAIL = mathewadvisement@gmail.com OWNER_PASSWORD = (pick a strong password) OWNER_NAME = Mathew Youhanna OWNER_BRAND = Meridian Life Group Railway sets PORT for you — don't add it. After saving, Railway redeploys.
7Open Settings → Networking → Generate Domain. You'll get a public URL like meridian-team-production.up.railway.app. That's your platform.

Part 3 — First sign-in

8Open your-url/ (the root). Sign in with the OWNER_EMAIL and OWNER_PASSWORD you set. Go to Settings and change your password.

Part 4 — Add your agents

9Click Add agent. Enter their name, email, phone, NPN, and tap their licensed states. Click Create agent.
10You'll get a welcome message with their password — copy it and send it to them. It has their card link, their dashboard link, and their login. Done. Repeat for everyone.
✅ Every agent automatically gets: their own card at your-url/a/their-name, a texted-link preview that unfurls as their own black/gold Meridian business card, and the Meridian crest as their home-screen icon. Nothing to generate per person — the server does it.

How the team uses it

Agents: open their card link on their phone → Share → Add to Home Screen (Meridian crest icon). They text their link to clients; it unfurls as their business card. They log into their dashboard to see who viewed it, quotes, and appointments.
You (owner): the Team tab shows every agent and their activity; Team inbox shows every quote/appointment across the whole team; you can reset a password, deactivate, or delete anyone.

Updating later

When I send you an updated version, upload the changed files to the same GitHub repo (or replace the folder). Railway redeploys automatically. Your Volume keeps all agent data.
