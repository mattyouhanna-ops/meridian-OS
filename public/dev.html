How to onboard someone

  1. Approve a request above (or just go straight to an agency).
  2. Open the Agencies tab → pick the agency → + Add agent.
  3. Enter their name, email, phone, carrier and states → Create agent.
  4. A welcome email with their login sends automatically — the password also shows under their name here so you can resend it.
  5. They open their card /a/their-name/admin, set their own password, and they're live.
